# Work4Tomorrow

Compare opportunities. Plan your next move.

## The Unique Kernel
Compare whether a job fits your demonstrated skills AND what it leaves you after work costs, then explain which opportunity to pursue and a salary discussion target. The recommendation is grounded in your profile and supplied job details; uniqueness against existing products still needs validation.

## Who It Is For
Entry-level and early-career job seekers across South Africa. The first demonstration compares retail and call-centre opportunities. Location does not limit the audience.

## The Core Loop
Open the app → create or reopen a profile → save jobs to a collection and select one to three for comparison → review and correct captured details → compare skills, costs and salary → save results → reopen and compare later.

## Inspiration & Identity
Chosen name: Work4Tomorrow. Calm, hopeful and professional visual direction in shades of blue. This page is a planning companion, not an approved app design. The next stage will establish the look and feel.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Build a useful competition entry while learning to read and understand code, preparing for app and game development. Target submission before 22 October 2026; fallback 24 October. Use GitHub SihleMpungose.

## What Working Looks Like
A user creates a skills-and-certifications profile, optionally extracts a local CV, sets desired salary and optional editable default transport costs, supplies retail and call-centre job details, reviews a comparison, saves it and reopens it on the same device. One job also works independently, and up to three can be compared. The memorable moment is seeing how skills fit, travel costs and time change the apparent value of a higher advertised salary.

## The POC Boundary
Reusable profile; optional CV upload; skills, certifications and desired salary; optional default transport costs with per-job edits; dashboard; saved job collection with no fixed job-count cap, subject to device storage; delete unwanted or expired jobs; select one to three jobs per comparison; manual and pasted job entry, local TXT/DOCX/text-based PDF extraction, source-link retention; reviewable captured fields; explained skills gaps, cost comparison, job recommendation and salary discussion target; saved comparisons on the same device. Input formats, extraction feasibility, missing-data behaviour and salary calculation assumptions must be resolved in requirements and technical planning. No guarantee that every job website can be read.

## Later
Online accounts and cross-device retrieval are deferred by the user. Broader features are not commitments. Any additional scope must be discussed before inclusion.

## Explicitly Cut
Ride safety is outside this project: the user chose a different problem. Cross-device login is outside the competition build: same-device saving is sufficient. Do not present missing salary or missing skills evidence as known facts, and do not invent qualifications or employer offers.

## Approved Scope Refinements — 18 October 2026
Mobile-friendly web app with same-browser/device saving. Optional current-job baseline outside the three prospective-job slots. Four priority factors: skills fit, money remaining, commuting time and working hours. Salary discussion range plus starting figure. Local core document processing; screenshot/scanned-PDF OCR and automatic job-link retrieval are stretch goals, alongside automatic tax calculations. See prd.md for the consolidated requirements.
